GeoBirdModel

3D models of tracked birds in flight · part of the GeoPressure suite

A 3D flying model of each bird species tracked with geolocators, made for the bird view of GeoLocator Explorer. Each bird flies over a habitat of its migration. The map shows one tracked bird of the species, its breeding and wintering sites and its long stopovers: click it to fly over another habitat, or press ▶ to follow the whole migration. In the map, As flown shows day or night as the bird flew there; most of these birds migrate at night.
