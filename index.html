Bird models

Flapping 3D models of the bird species tracked with multi-sensor geolocators, made for the bird view of GeoLocator Explorer. Click a bird to see it fly, turn it around and download it. Free to reuse under CC BY 4.0.

Use a model

Every model is a glTF binary (.glb, about 2 MB) with one looping animation, Fly (one wing beat per second). Head along +Y, back along +Z (glTF axes), wingspan 0.5 units. All models are listed in models.json; files are served through the jsDelivr CDN:

https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/GeoPressure/bird-models@main/models/Upupa_epops.glb

In a web page with <model-viewer>:

<script type="module" src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/@google/model-viewer@3.5.0/dist/model-viewer.min.js"></script>
<model-viewer src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/GeoPressure/bird-models@main/models/Upupa_epops.glb"
  animation-name="Fly" autoplay camera-controls></model-viewer>

In three.js: load with GLTFLoader and play gltf.animations[0] with an AnimationMixer.

Models by GeoPressure/bird-models (Raphaël Nussbaumer, Swiss Ornithological Institute), made from the huashu-bird-sticker illustrations by Huashu (CC BY 4.0) with Meshy and Blender; method in GeoLocatorExplorer/bird-models.
